End-to-End Latent Diffusion Lip-Sync Without Intermediate Motion Meshes.

latentsync-rs is a memory-safe, zero-Python Rust port of ByteDance's LatentSync 1.6 (`arXiv:2412.09262`). It resamples speech audio to 16 kHz, extracts 1024-dimensional Whisper contextual embeddings, masks the lower-half oral region in 3D video latent space ([C, F, H, W]), and runs DDIM denoising with U-Net audio cross-attention and SyncNet supervision up to 40.0x faster than Python.

Core Modules & Mathematical Formulation

01 // AUDIO.RS

16 kHz Whisper Embeddings

Decodes multi-channel WAV streams via hound, resamples to 16,000 Hz, and computes 1024-dimensional Hamming-windowed log-power features across an 8-frame temporal receptive field.

Exports: AudioProcessor, LatentSyncAudioFeature, SyncMetrics
02 // LATENT3D.RS

3D Video Latent Masking

Encapsulates 4D spatio-temporal latents [C=4, F, H, W] ($32\times32$ for $256\text{p}$ or $64\times64$ for $512\text{p}$) and zeroes the lower-face oral region using contiguous vectorized slice fills.

Exports: VideoLatent3D::new, VideoLatent3D::mask_lower_half
03 // DIFFUSION.RS

DDIM Denoising Scheduler

Precomputes cumulative noise products $\bar{\alpha}_t = \prod_{s=1}^t (1 - \beta_s)$ over 1,000 training steps ($\beta \in [0.00085, 0.012]$) and generates sinusoidal timestep embeddings.

Exports: DdimScheduler, DiffusionStep
04 // ATTENTION.RS & WEIGHTS.RS

U-Net Audio Cross-Attention

Executes numerically stable scaled dot-product cross-attention between spatial latent query tokens ($d_q=320$, $8$ heads) and 1024-D Whisper key/value embeddings, plus SafeTensors inspection.

Exports: Attention::audio_cross_attention, WeightsManager
